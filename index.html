About Our Academy

ABOUT OUR
SUN FLOWER ACADEMY

Established in 2004 in Santir Bazar, South Tripura, SUN FLOWER ENGLISH MEDIUM ACADEMY H.S (+2 STAGE) stands as a beacon of quality English-medium education. Under the visionary leadership of Principal Sri Gopal Mallik Sir, the academy provides comprehensive Higher Secondary (+2 Stage) education in both Science and Arts streams.

Our curriculum blends rigorous board exam preparation, modern science laboratory experiments, literary enrichment, and personal mentorship to empower students for higher education and civic leadership.
